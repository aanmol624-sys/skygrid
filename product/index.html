The Product

What does UFV receive for $100M?

On September 1, 2030, UFV receives an operational autonomous-aviation ecosystem — not merely infrastructure. SkyGrid is a system of systems.

On this page

  • System of systems
  • Field environments

14 finished deliverables

Hover, focus or tap a card to see what UFV receives.

  1. 01

    Innovation & Simulation Node

    What UFV receives

    Commissioned central capability for simulation, mission planning, prototyping, secure compute/data, research, teaching, and partner collaboration.

  2. 02

    Fraser Valley Field-Test Network

    What UFV receives

    At least three proposed-target compliant field environments representing different operating needs; exact sites determined through feasibility.

    Proposed target PROPOSED / TEAM DESIGN: Numbers such as the three-site target, eight-partner target, five-program target, budget allocation, change thresholds, risk scores, and KPI targets are SkyGrid planning choices created by the team. They are not presented as commitments made by UFV, government, regulators, or external partners.
  3. 03

    Modular Autonomous-Aircraft Fleet

    What UFV receives

    Controlled portfolio selected from validated missions and regulatory requirements; no invented aircraft count.

  4. 04

    Sensor & Payload Library

    What UFV receives

    Flexible optical, mapping, multispectral, thermal, environmental, positioning/navigation, and research payload capability as justified.

  5. 05

    Simulation / Mission-Control / Data Platform

    What UFV receives

    Integrated pre-flight simulation/planning, authorized operational monitoring, secure data transfer, storage, processing, analytics, and visualization.

  6. 06

    Safety & Regulatory Operating System

    What UFV receives

    Operating categories, qualifications, SMS, SOPs, maintenance, incident/emergency procedures, authorization and insurance/liability processes.

  7. 07

    Cybersecurity, Privacy & Data Governance

    What UFV receives

    System access, network/C2 security, firmware/software management, partner/research data rules, imagery/personal-data controls, retention/sharing/deletion, incident response.

  8. 08

    Student Applied-Learning Program

    What UFV receives

    Formal routes through courses, capstones, co-op/practica, research assistantships, multidisciplinary challenges and partner projects.

  9. 09

    Applied Research Program

    What UFV receives

    Clear route from concept → simulation → prototype → approved field test → analysis → research/partner output.

  10. 10

    Industry & Community Challenge Program

    What UFV receives

    Defined intake process beginning with partner problem, not “we have a drone — what can we sell you?”

  11. 11

    Strategic Partnership Portfolio

    What UFV receives

    Proposed launch target ≥8 substantive written external agreements across more than one sector/type.

    Proposed target PROPOSED / TEAM DESIGN: Numbers such as the three-site target, eight-partner target, five-program target, budget allocation, change thresholds, risk scores, and KPI targets are SkyGrid planning choices created by the team. They are not presented as commitments made by UFV, government, regulators, or external partners.
  12. 12

    Trained Operating Organization

    What UFV receives

    All necessary operational/safety/technical/academic/partner functions staffed or assigned before launch; exact headcount determined from workload.

  13. 13

    Sustainable Operating Model

    What UFV receives

    Five-year operating plan, staffing/maintenance, technology refresh, funding mix, lifecycle plan, governance and benefits review.

  14. 14

    Tested Integrated Capability

    What UFV receives

    Representative end-to-end missions prove people, technology, field sites, safety, data and partner/student workflows operate as a coherent system.

A system of systems

Infrastructure + Technology + People + Programs + Partnerships + Governance = SkyGrid
  • A finished building without field sites is incomplete;
  • field sites without technology/data systems are incomplete;
  • technology without trained people is incomplete;
  • and equipment without academic/research pathways does not make UFV meaningfully better.
