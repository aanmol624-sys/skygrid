SkyGrid will use a proposed 5×5 probability–impact model. The numerical model is a prioritization aid, not permission to ignore low-probability catastrophic events. Safety-critical risks receive mandatory controls regardless of arithmetic score. Table 12 lists the proposed risk register; scores are planning judgments, not measured probabilities.

8.1 Cybersecurity and privacy controls

Cybersecurity

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security recommends conducting a threat risk assessment before deploying a drone in order to identify security threats and appropriate mitigations for systems, data, and networks . SkyGrid therefore proposes a platform-onboarding security review covering:

  • Supplier risk
  • Command-and-control architecture
  • Firmware/software
  • Network segmentation
  • Access controls
  • Logging
  • Incident-response integration

Privacy

The Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada notes that Canadian privacy law can apply when organizations use drones to collect or use personal information and emphasizes purpose, consent where applicable, limiting collection, safeguards, and retention principles . SkyGrid’s project-specific design principle:

  • Collect the minimum data required for an approved purpose
  • Define capture boundaries
  • Retention
  • Access
  • Sharing
  • Deletion — all defined before operations
