Why SkyGrid · Success & KPIs

Success Criteria, KPIs & Benefits Realization

Finishing SkyGrid is not the same as succeeding with SkyGrid.

On this page

  • Benefits chain
  • Launch acceptance
  • KPI dashboard
  • North Star

PMI’s Benefits Realization Management framework distinguishes delivering project outputs from realizing and sustaining benefits after those outputs move into operations . SkyGrid therefore measures two levels of success: project delivery success at launch, and benefits realization after launch.

Proposed launch acceptance criteria

Proposed PROPOSED / TEAM DESIGN: Numbers such as the three-site target, eight-partner target, five-program target, budget allocation, change thresholds, risk scores, and KPI targets are SkyGrid planning choices created by the team. They are not presented as commitments made by UFV, government, regulators, or external partners.
  • Schedule
    Operational launch no later than Sep. 1, 2030; internal target acceptance Aug. 15.
  • Cost
    Total authorization ≤ CAD $100M.
  • Central capability
    Innovation & Simulation Node commissioned and integrated.
  • Field network
    ≥3 compliant field-test environments/sites operational.
  • Partnerships
    ≥8 substantive written external agreements.
  • Academic integration
    Participation pathways across ≥5 UFV academic/program areas.
  • Technology
    Core RPAS, payload, simulation, communications, compute/data and analytics systems commissioned.
  • Safety / regulation
    Required controls/permissions for launch operations and no unresolved critical safety deficiency.
  • Cyber / privacy
    No unresolved critical cybersecurity or privacy/data-governance deficiency affecting launch.
  • People
    Safety-critical launch roles staffed and qualified.
  • Operations
    Representative end-to-end pilot operations and readiness review completed.
  • Handover
    Permanent operating owner formally accepts the capability.

SAFE & SUSTAIN

S1–S5

Serious safety events; reporting culture; critical cyber findings; regulatory compliance; system availability

Zero unresolved critical launch findings; zero knowingly unauthorized operations; PROPOSED mature availability ≥95%.

✦ Proposed North Star student-benefit metric

Number of UFV students annually completing a field-validated or external-partner SkyGrid experience.

SkyGrid should establish 2027 baselines before claiming percentage improvements in student participation, relevant applied research, external funding, or partner activity. A percentage improvement without a baseline would be misleading.

Metrics we refuse to use as strategic success

  • Number of drones purchased
  • Square feet constructed
  • Meetings held
  • Companies contacted
  • Website views
  • Press releases
  • Gross revenue without context

Those can be operational statistics but they do not prove educational, research, regional, safety, or sustainability benefits.
