High-level scope

What the project includes and what it explicitly excludes (Table 3).

IN SCOPE

  • Central research, simulation, and collaboration capability.
  • Regulatory-compliant regional field-test locations.
  • RPAS platforms, payloads, sensors, command-and-control, and simulation systems.
  • Secure compute, data, networking, cybersecurity, and privacy controls.
  • Academic and applied-research integration.
  • Student applied-learning pathways.
  • Industry/community challenge process and external partnerships.
  • Staffing, operator readiness, safety management, regulatory compliance, insurance, commissioning, and handover.

OUT OF SCOPE

  • ⁄⁄A commercial airline or conventional flight school.
  • ⁄⁄Replacement of Transport Canada, NAV CANADA, airport-authority, or other regulatory functions.
  • ⁄⁄Weaponized or offensive military drone development.
  • ⁄⁄Passenger-carrying autonomous-aircraft services.
  • ⁄⁄Unrestricted BVLOS operations outside approved Canadian regulatory pathways.
  • ⁄⁄Routine independent emergency-response operations by UFV.
  • ⁄⁄Mass manufacturing of certified aircraft.

3.1 Major deliverables

  1. 01Approved governance and project-management framework.
  2. 02Feasibility and site strategy.
  3. 03Commissioned central Innovation & Simulation Node.
  4. 04Commissioned Fraser Valley Field-Test Network.
  5. 05Aircraft, sensors, simulation, computing, and data infrastructure.
  6. 06Safety, regulatory, privacy, cybersecurity, insurance, and incident-response framework.
  7. 07Interdisciplinary academic and applied-research pathways.
  8. 08External partnership and Challenge Program model.
  9. 09Trained operating organization.
  10. 10Integrated testing, operational-readiness review, handover, and September 1, 2030 launch.
