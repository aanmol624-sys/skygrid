The Plan · Schedule

Schedule & Gantt

The WBS defines what SkyGrid must deliver; the schedule converts those outputs into sequence, duration, dependencies, milestones, and accountability. The external deadline remains September 1, 2030, but the proposed internal operational-acceptance target is August 15, 2030 so the team does not plan to finish on the deadline itself.

7.2 Schedule control

Proposed triggers PROPOSED / TEAM DESIGN: Numbers such as the three-site target, eight-partner target, five-program target, budget allocation, change thresholds, risk scores, and KPI targets are SkyGrid planning choices created by the team. They are not presented as commitments made by UFV, government, regulators, or external partners.
Green

Trigger<10 working days variance and no milestone impact.

Management responseWorkstream/PM correction.

Amber

Trigger10–30 working days variance OR risk to a major milestone.

Management responseFormal recovery plan.

Red

Trigger>30 working days, gate date threatened, or Sep. 1 forecast affected.

Management responseEscalate to Steering Committee / Sponsor.

Proposed recovery sequence

  1. 1 Resequence unaffected work
  2. 2 Parallelize compatible work
  3. 3 Add resources where that genuinely shortens duration
  4. 4 Remove Category-C enhancements
  5. 5 Defer noncritical capability
  6. 6 Formally rebaseline only through approved change control

Safety and regulatory requirements are not compressed merely to protect the launch date.
