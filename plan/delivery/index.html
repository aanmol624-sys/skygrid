The Plan · Delivery & Gates

Delivery Approach — Prove Before We Spend

Core control principle: SkyGrid does not receive an unconditional $100M spending commitment on Day 1. Each major investment stage must earn permission to proceed.

A hybrid, stage-gated delivery approach

PMI describes hybrid delivery as combining predictive and adaptive practices for projects with different degrees of certainty and change .

PREDICTIVE LANE

Where requirements must be controlled

FacilitiesSitesMajor capital procurementRegulatory deliverablesBudgetsMilestone dates

ADAPTIVE LANE

Work that will evolve 2026–2030

SoftwareAnalyticsPrototypesResearch use casesPartner pilotsSelected technology configurations

Jun 2027

Gate 1 — Business & feasibility

Prove Need

✓ CONTINUE only if…

Credible applications; external interest; plausible regulatory/site path; preliminary affordability; no fatal legal/safety/site barrier.

↻ REDIRECT / ✕ STOP — examples

Narrow use cases, move node, change operating category, or terminate if there is no credible demand/path.

Dec 2027

Gate 2 — Site & regulatory

Prove Permission

✓ CONTINUE only if…

Central-node solution viable; ≥3 field-environment pathways plausible; regulatory strategy documented; rights-holder/site concerns understood; updated cost viable.

↻ REDIRECT / ✕ STOP — examples

Change sites, reduce operational complexity, or stop before detailed design.

Jun 2028

Gate 3 — Capital investment

Prove Affordability

✓ CONTINUE only if…

Design mature enough for reliable costing; total authorization ≤$100M incl. reserves; site agreements advanced; regulatory path viable; tech specs fit intended missions.

↻ REDIRECT / ✕ STOP — examples

Value engineer, cut optional scope, or do not authorize major construction/fleet purchase.

Dec 2029

Gate 4 — Capability

Prove Capability

✓ CONTINUE only if…

Major infrastructure substantially complete; field network/core technology established; academic/safety/cyber/privacy systems functioning; launch forecast credible.

↻ REDIRECT / ✕ STOP — examples

Redirect capability, defer optional equipment/use cases, consolidate sites.

Jun 30, 2030

Gate 5 — Operational readiness

Prove Readiness

✓ CONTINUE only if…

Required permissions; trained people; tested safety/cyber/data controls; field sites; reliable core systems; academic/partner readiness; no critical deficiency.

↻ REDIRECT / ✕ STOP — examples

Continue, limited launch, correct and retest, or do not launch unsafe/unapproved capability.

6.3 Proposed wave procurement

Team design PROPOSED / TEAM DESIGN: Numbers such as the three-site target, eight-partner target, five-program target, budget allocation, change thresholds, risk scores, and KPI targets are SkyGrid planning choices created by the team. They are not presented as commitments made by UFV, government, regulators, or external partners.

The three-wave structure is a team design decision intended to reduce technology-obsolescence risk; it is not a requirement imposed by Transport Canada or PMI.

28

Wave 1 · 2028

Small core fleet for training, integration, system validation, and early staff readiness.

29

Wave 2 · 2029

Mission-specific systems selected only after use cases and operating requirements have been validated.

30

Wave 3 · 2030

Targeted final acquisitions only where readiness testing identifies a genuine launch need.
